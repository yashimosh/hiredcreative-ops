# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Initial release

Fork of [career-ops](https://github.com/santifer/career-ops), retargeted at creative
professionals (Creative/Art Directors, brand and product designers, copywriters,
motion and content roles) instead of AI/automation careers.

- Renamed to hiredcreative-ops throughout (skills, commands, package metadata, dashboard)
- Default archetypes, proof-point framing, and portal examples reworked for the creative industry
- Ships a pre-wired integration with [Hired Creative](https://hiredcreative.com), a free aggregator of remote creative jobs
- Trimmed to the core job-search engine (evaluation, batch processing, portal scanning, CV generation, application tracking, comp negotiation) — the upstream project's client-CRM and personal-brand-content extensions are out of scope for this fork
